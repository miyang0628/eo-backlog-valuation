# Contracted Data Revenue and Equity Valuation — Replication Package

Replication code, data, and manuscript for the study
*"Contracted Data Revenue and Equity Valuation: Backlog Depth as an
Information Signal in Listed Earth-Observation Firms."*

> **Anonymized for peer review.** Author, affiliation, funding, and venue
> details are withheld in accordance with the double-blind review policy of
> *[Anonymous Journal]*. A fully attributed version will accompany the
> camera-ready manuscript.

## Research question

Alternative-data firms increasingly sell *recurring, contracted* data
subscriptions rather than one-off hardware or imagery. This paper asks a
single empirical question: **is the depth of an Earth-observation (EO) firm's
contracted-but-unrecognized data revenue reflected in its equity valuation?**
If satellite data carries leading information for the firms that buy it
(*information production*, taken as given from the literature), does the
market in turn reflect the *backlog* of that data business in the provider's
own multiple (*information capitalization*)?

We operationalize contracted data-revenue depth as **backlog depth** =
remaining performance obligations (RPO) scaled by trailing revenue, and
relate `log(EV/Sales)` to backlog depth and revenue growth under firm and
year fixed effects.

This is a within-cohort **association** study on a small, young panel (three
firms, 40 firm-quarters). It makes no causal claim, and — as the robustness
analysis shows openly — with only three clusters conventional significance is
not attainable. We report that rather than hide it.

## Headline result

Across four nested specifications on 40 firm-quarters (three firms,
2022–2026), backlog depth carries a **positive, sign-stable** coefficient: it
is positive in all four specifications, positive within every firm separately
(per-firm slopes 0.14 / 0.70 / 1.41), and positive under within-firm demeaning.
A one-standard-deviation increase in backlog depth corresponds to roughly a
50% higher EV/Sales multiple. Under heteroskedasticity-robust errors the
preferred coefficient is significant at the 5% level; because the panel has
only three clusters we lead on sign stability rather than cluster-robust
*p*-values. Controlling for revenue directly (or moving it to the RHS with
log(EV) as the outcome) *strengthens* the coefficient, ruling out a mechanical
shared-denominator explanation; a per-share formulation is insignificant and is
reported openly. A within-firm changes test suggests increases in backlog depth
precede multiple re-rating in the following quarter (directional, *p* ≈ 0.09).

## Manuscript

`manuscript.tex` is the full paper in Elsevier `elsarticle` format (author-year
/ Harvard references), double-blind ready. Compile with:

```bash
pdflatex manuscript
bibtex   manuscript
pdflatex manuscript
pdflatex manuscript
```

All data figures and numbers in the manuscript are generated by
`analyze_info_capitalization.py` and included from `results/figures/`. The
schematic figures (graphical abstract, information-flow diagram, backlog
definition, and the four appendix concept figures) are in `figures_concept/`.
The graphical abstract is placed in the Elsevier front matter; Figures 1–2
(information flow, backlog definition) are in the body; Figures 3–7 are the data
figures; Figures A.1–A.4 are the appendix concept figures. Every reference in
`references.bib` was verified against publisher/Crossref metadata.

## Reproduce

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python build_eo_panel.py               # (optional) rebuild data/eo_panel_refined.csv from SEC
python analyze_info_capitalization.py  # regenerate every table and figure in results/
```

`build_eo_panel.py` pulls quarterly revenue, remaining performance
obligations, shares outstanding, debt, and cash from SEC company-concept
endpoints, joins quarter-end prices, and constructs EV/Sales. Prices are
downloaded live, so exact values depend on the retrieval date; the SEC
accounting facts are stable. `analyze_info_capitalization.py` runs entirely
offline on the committed `eo_panel_refined.csv` and prints the same numbers
it writes to `results/`.

## Outputs

- **`t1_panel_descriptives.csv`** — firm-level means/SDs of EV/Sales, backlog
  depth, and growth.
- **`t2_panel_regressions.csv`** — the four nested specifications (pooled,
  firm FE, year FE, firm+year FE), coefficients, firm-clustered SEs, and
  *p*-values.
- **`t3_robustness.csv`** — HC1 vs clustered SEs, leave-one-firm-out
  re-estimation, and within-firm demeaning.
- **`t4_associations.csv`** — Pearson and Spearman correlations of each
  signal with the multiple.
- **`t5_denominator_defense.csv`** — shared-denominator diagnostics: controlling
  for log revenue, log(EV) as outcome, and a per-share formulation.
- **`t6_controls_and_changes.csv`** — liquidity (cash/revenue) control, and
  within-firm changes and lead specifications.
- **`t7_per_firm_slopes.csv`** — backlog-depth slope estimated firm by firm
  (Ibragimov–Müller-style sign-stability evidence).
- **Figures 1–2 (body, schematic)** — information-flow diagram; backlog-depth
  definition.
- **Figures 3–7 (body, data)** — backlog-vs-multiple scatter with pooled fit;
  coefficient across specifications; backlog-depth timelines by firm;
  leave-one-firm-out coefficient stability; per-firm slopes.
- **Figures A.1–A.4 (appendix, schematic)** — hypothesis DAG; research-design
  pipeline; shared-denominator defense; sample structure.
- **Graphical abstract** — placed in the Elsevier front matter.

## Data provenance

- **Prices** — public market-data API; reproducible up to retrieval date.
- **Accounting facts** — SEC XBRL company-concept endpoints (official).
- **Shares** — reported common shares, with diluted weighted-average shares
  as a fallback where the instant count is not filed.

No values are imputed. Firm-quarters missing any regression component are
dropped, never filled. Satellogic (SATL) never discloses RPO and therefore
does not enter the regression; we state this rather than backfill a proxy.

## Scope and limitations

- The regression panel has three firms and 40 firm-quarters. Clustered
  inference on three clusters is fragile; the leave-one-firm-out analysis
  shows the coefficient is not driven by a single firm's sign but does lose
  conventional significance under some deletions. Estimates are associations
  within this cohort, not identified causal effects.
- The cohort is young, growth-oriented, and de-SPAC'd; historical share
  counts would tighten EV construction.
- EV/Sales uses period-end shares (with a diluted-WA fallback) and disclosed
  debt/cash.

## Relationship to prior work

This package shares its underlying four-firm panel and SEC pipeline with a
separate methodological study that used the same pipeline as a *governance
test bed* and treated its statistical content as incidental. The present
study is disjoint in aim: it takes the valuation question itself as the
object of analysis — the information-capitalization hypothesis — and adds the
robustness and association analyses (Tables 3–4, Figures 2–4) needed to
assess it. No governance, auditability, or explainability material is
reused here.

## Ethical scope

All inputs are public, firm-level, and aggregate. The design excludes any
personal, individual-identifying, or non-public firm-specific pre-disclosure
data by construction.
